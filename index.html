Privacy Support

The problem

You meet someone. They say their name. Five seconds later it is gone — and after that, asking again feels worse every time you put it off.

What the app does

It hands them the phone. They record their own name, in their own voice, the way they actually say it. Later you tap play and hear it exactly right, before you walk into the room.

  • They record it themselves — under 20 seconds, start to finish
  • A selfie, an emoji or a note about where you met
  • Groups for a class, a team, a gym or a company
  • Search by name, by sound or by group
  • Keep the raw audio file if you want it
  • Back everyone up to a folder in Files. The recordings are plain audio files you can play without the app

Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no sign-up, no servers, no analytics, no third-party code. The app cannot send your people anywhere because it has no networking in it at all.

Price

Free for up to 20 people, with no features held back. One payment of $1.99 unlocks unlimited. It is not a subscription, and your unlock follows your Apple ID to any device you own.
