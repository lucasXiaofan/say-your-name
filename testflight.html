What you are installing

Say Your Name is an app for remembering how people's names are actually pronounced. You hand someone your phone, they say their own name out loud, and later you play it back and get it right.

It is not finished yet, which is what a beta means. You are trying it early so the parts that annoy you can be fixed before everyone else sees them.

How to install it

Apple does not let unreleased apps go through the normal App Store, so beta versions arrive through a separate Apple app called TestFlight. You install TestFlight once, and betas appear inside it.

1. Install TestFlight

Open the App Store on your iPhone, search for TestFlight, and install it. It is made by Apple and it is free. Do not search the App Store for Say Your Name — it is not there yet.

2. Open the invite link

Tap the link above on your iPhone. TestFlight opens.

3. Tap Accept, then Install

The app installs onto your home screen like any other app. An orange dot next to its name just means it is a beta build.

4. Open it and add someone

Tap Add in the bottom-right corner, then hand your phone to whoever is with you. They hold the microphone, say their own name, and check the spelling. A selfie, an emoji and a group are all optional. A recording or a name on its own is enough to save.

Things worth knowing

  • It is free during the beta. If you try the $1.99 unlock, TestFlight shows a purchase sheet but never charges you. It is a test purchase, and it does not carry over to the App Store version.
  • Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no servers. See the privacy policy — the app has no networking code in it at all.
  • Each build expires after 90 days. TestFlight will offer you a newer one before that happens.
  • Updates arrive through TestFlight, not the App Store, for as long as you are on the beta.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored, unless you first saved a copy with Settings › Back up to Files. Moving from the beta to the App Store version keeps your people. Only deleting the app loses them.

Telling me what is wrong

This is the entire point of a beta, so please do it.

Fastest way: take a screenshot of the problem, then in TestFlight open the app and tap Send Beta Feedback. The screenshot and the technical details go along with it automatically.

Or just email lucaswe957@gmail.com.

Useful things to say: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. "The recording button did nothing when I tapped it the second time" is worth more than "it's broken." Confusing wording and anything that felt slow are real bugs too — tell me about those.
